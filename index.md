## The Zalgo deployment

A registry of every render subject in both Minecraft editions — Java Edition and
Pocket Edition (Bedrock) — marked so you can tell **what kind of thing** each one
is without reading the row.

Marks above the letters mean the subject is 3D modelled. Marks below mean it is
shaded. Marks on both sides mean it resolves geometry and shading together. The
heavier the marks, the less of the subject a resource pack can reach.
